=====================================================================
  PicklePOS  -  Point of Sale for Pickleball Courts & Cafe
  Setup guide for the cafe laptop + POS-5890U-L 58mm receipt printer
=====================================================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  - The PicklePOS folder (this folder)
  - Google Chrome (best) or Microsoft Edge on the laptop
  - The POS-5890U-L thermal printer, its USB cable and power adapter
  - No internet is needed after setup. Everything runs on the laptop.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - Copy the app to the laptop
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Copy the whole "PicklePOS" folder to the laptop, for example to
     C:\PicklePOS   (any location is fine; don't use a USB stick).
     You don't need to copy the "test-data" folder; it only holds
     practice data from "Test POS.bat".
     Or download it fresh from GitHub:
       https://github.com/ljdstechva/PicklePOS
       (green "Code" button > Download ZIP). Right-click the zip >
       Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, then extract it.
  2. Right-click "Start POS.bat" > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).
     Rename the desktop shortcut to "PicklePOS".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - Make sure Chrome is installed
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Windows 10/11: install Google Chrome from google.com/chrome
    (Edge also works if Chrome isn't installed.)
  - Windows 7/8: the last Chrome version for Windows 7 is 109, and it
    still works with PicklePOS.
  - To check your Windows version: press the Windows key + R, type
    winver and press Enter.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - Install the receipt printer
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Plug the printer's power adapter in, load the 58mm paper roll
     (shiny side facing the print head), and turn the printer ON.
  2. Connect the USB cable to the laptop.
  3. On the laptop, open  www.cnfujun.com/d/50  and download the
     printer driver (from the printer's manual). Run the installer
     and click "Next". The printer must be ON and plugged in, or
     the installer cannot finish.
  4. Open Windows Settings > Devices (or Bluetooth & devices) >
     Printers & scanners. You should see a printer named like
     "POS-58" or "POS58 Printer".
       - Click it > "Set as default".
       - Windows 10/11: also turn OFF "Let Windows manage my
         default printer".
  5. Click the printer > Printing preferences (or Printer properties
     > Preferences > Advanced) and set Paper Size to the 58mm roll
     size, e.g. "58mm x 3276mm" or "58(48) x 210mm".
  6. Print a Windows test page to confirm the printer works.

  Using Bluetooth instead of USB (optional; USB is more reliable):
    - Turn the printer on, open Windows Bluetooth settings, add the
      device (PIN is usually 0000 or 1234).
    - Open Devices and Printers > right-click POS-58 > Printer
      properties > Ports > tick the "Standard Serial over Bluetooth
      link" COM port > Apply.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - Open PicklePOS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click the "PicklePOS" desktop shortcut (Start POS.bat).
     The app opens in its own full window.
     ALWAYS open the app this way. Opening index.html directly will
     show an empty app, because your data lives in the "data" folder
     that only the shortcut uses. Start POS.bat also turns on silent
     printing, so receipts print without a dialog box.
  2. First time only (2 short steps):
       Step 1: business name, business logo (it becomes the app icon
               and is printed on receipts), owner name and owner PIN.
               Keep the sample menu if you want to try things out.
       Step 2: add your employees - a name and PIN for each, and what
               they may use. "Standard" = take orders, open tabs,
               Kitchen screen and Stock. You can skip this and add
               employees later in Settings > Staff & PINs.
  3. Go to Settings and fill in:
       - Business & Receipt: address, contact no., TIN
       - Courts & Rates: court names, price per hour, billing rules
  4. Settings > Printer > "Print Test Receipt".
       - Text too small or too big?  Change "Font size".
       - Paper cut too close?  Increase "Blank space after".
       - A print dialog pops up?  Close ALL PicklePOS windows and
         open it again with the shortcut.
       - Prints on the wrong printer?  Set POS-58 as the default
         printer (Step 3.4).


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - Add your menu with photos
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Menu > + Add Item
    - Choose Photo: any phone photo works. It is shrunk to a small
      size automatically so the laptop stays fast.
    - Tick "Send to the kitchen" for items the kitchen/barista
      prepares (coffee, food). Leave it off for bottled drinks etc.
    - Tick "Out of stock right now" if you don't have it today.
    - Untick "Show on the Sell screen" to hide an item without
      deleting it (e.g. out of season).
  Categories: add, rename and reorder them on the left.
  Each item has an In stock / Out of stock switch (no counting).


---------------------------------------------------------------------
DAILY USE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  CAFE ORDER, PAY NOW
    Sell > tap items > Pay > choose Cash / GCash / Maya / Bank >
    enter amount > Complete Sale. The receipt prints, and the food and
    drinks to prepare appear on the Kitchen screen. Type the
    customer's name in the Payment window so the kitchen can call it.

  OPEN TAB (order now, pay later)
    Tap items > "To Kitchen" (sends the order to the Kitchen screen
    and saves the tab) or "Save Tab" (saves without sending). Find it
    later under "Tabs" and press Pay.

  KITCHEN SCREEN (the "Kitchen" button in the left side bar)
    The printer only prints customer receipts. Orders to prepare are
    shown as cards on the Kitchen screen instead:
      NEW       -> press "Start Preparing"
      PREPARING -> tap each item when it's done (it gets a tick),
                   then press "Ready"
      READY     -> appears on the right; press "Served" once it's
                   handed over ("<-" moves it back if pressed early)
    - The waiting time turns orange after 10 minutes and red after 20.
    - ADD-ON = extra items added to a tab that was already sent.
    - Items removed from an order, or a voided order, show in red as
      CANCELLED. Don't prepare them; press "OK, Remove".
    - "Recently Served" lists today's served orders; "Recall" puts one
      back on the board.
    - The number on the Kitchen button = orders still to prepare.
    Prefer paper? Settings > Printer > Kitchen orders can be set to
    print a kitchen slip, or both screen and slip.

  OUT OF STOCK
    Stock (left side bar) > tap the item. It turns red, shows
    "Out of stock" on the Sell screen and cannot be ordered. Tap it
    again when it is back. "All Back in Stock" resets everything.
    Menu > Stock History shows who changed what. Reports > "Out of
    Stock List" prints a list for restocking.

  COURT RENTAL
    Courts > "Start Timer" when players start, or "Book Hours" for a
    fixed reservation. Press "Stop Timer" when they finish; the court
    fee goes onto their tab together with any food and drinks, and
    "Take Payment Now" collects it all at once.
    Billing rules (Settings > Courts): e.g. minimum 60 min, then
    every 30 min, with 5 min grace. The Courts page shows the rule.
    Forgot to start the timer? Change the start time in the Start
    Timer window, or ask the owner to edit it (the 3-dot button).

  SENIOR CITIZEN / PWD DISCOUNT
    Discount > Senior Citizen or PWD > enter total guests, how many
    are Senior/PWD, the name and the ID number. The discount is 20%
    of their share. The receipt shows the ID and a signature line.

  SPLIT PAYMENT
    In the Payment window, enter part of the amount (e.g. GCash 500)
    > "Add Partial Payment", then pay the rest by another method.

  VOIDS & REFUNDS
    History > click the sale > Void Sale (needs the owner PIN,
    unless the owner allowed that employee to void).

  END OF DAY
    Reports > "Close Day & Backup". This prints the Z-Reading and
    saves a backup file to the Downloads folder. Count the cash
    drawer and write it on the Z-Reading.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
BACKUPS - PLEASE READ
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  All data (sales, menu, photos, settings) is stored on this laptop
  in  PicklePOS\data\ . If the laptop breaks, the data is gone,
  unless you have a backup.
    - A backup file is saved to Downloads every time you use
      "Close Day & Backup" (or Settings > Download Backup).
      A yellow "Backup due" button appears if you skip a day.
    - Once a week, copy the newest PicklePOS-backup-....json file
      to a USB drive, Google Drive or Messenger (send it to yourself).
    - New laptop: copy the PicklePOS folder, open it, finish the
      first setup, then Settings > Restore from Backup.
  Do NOT delete the "data" folder, and do not run Chrome's "Clear
  browsing data" inside the PicklePOS window.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
ABOUT RECEIPTS & BIR
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  PicklePOS is not a BIR-accredited CAS/POS. Its printouts are
  "Acknowledgement Receipts - Not an Official Receipt" for the
  customer's reference. Keep issuing BIR-registered invoices/receipts
  from your booklet when required. You can change the wording in
  Settings > Business & Receipt.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
HANDY EXTRAS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Keyboard shortcuts (Sell screen):
    F2 = search    Enter = add the first search result
    F4 = court time    F8 = save tab    F9 = pay
    Enter = confirm a window    Esc = close it
  Removed an item by mistake? Press UNDO on the message at the bottom.
  The small number on a dish photo shows how many are in the order.
  Courts screen: the bar under the timer shows when the next court
  charge starts. Booked courts warn (with a beep) 5 minutes before the
  end and when time is up.
  Settings > Display & Sounds:
    - Animations on/off (turn off if the laptop feels slow)
    - Sounds on/off
    - Auto-lock after 5-30 minutes of no use. Locking keeps the
      current order on screen.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAFF & ACCESS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Owner:    everything.
  Employee: only what the owner ticks in Settings > Staff & PINs >
            Edit. "Standard" = Take orders, Open tabs, Kitchen screen,
            Stock. Other screens: Courts, Sales history, Reports,
            Menu, Settings.
  Extra permissions (otherwise they ask for the owner PIN): void
  sales & tabs, custom discounts, change/remove court time, change
  prices on an order.
  Staff accounts and backups are always owner-only.
  Press "Lock" (your initials, bottom left) when leaving the counter.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
AUTOMATIC UPDATES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  UPDATE BUTTON (anyone, including employees): press "Update" at the
  bottom of the left side bar. If the laptop has internet, PicklePOS
  asks GitHub for a newer version, downloads it and installs it by
  itself - the window closes and opens again in a few seconds.
  Finish or save the current order first. The same button is in
  Settings > About & Updates ("Update Now").

  When the laptop has internet, PicklePOS also checks GitHub for a new
  version by itself (a few seconds after opening, then every 3 hours)
  and downloads it quietly in the background.
    - A dark bar appears: "PicklePOS x.y.z is ready". Press
      "Restart Now" (finish the current order first) - the window
      closes and opens again with the new version. Or press "Later";
      it installs the next time PicklePOS is opened.
    - Your sales, menu, photos and settings are kept (the "data"
      folder is never changed by an update).
    - Settings > About & Updates shows the version and lets the
      owner "Check for Updates Now".
    - Updates only work when PicklePOS is opened with the shortcut
      (Start POS.bat). Details are logged in updates\update-log.txt.
    - The previous version is kept in updates\previous.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Nothing prints     - Is the printer ON, with paper and the USB in?
                       Is POS-58 the default printer? Try a Windows
                       test page first.
  Faint print        - Thermal paper is loaded upside down (flip it),
                       or the paper is old.
  App looks empty    - You opened index.html directly. Close it and
                       use the PicklePOS shortcut.
  Forgot owner PIN   - If another owner account exists, log in with it
                       and reset the PIN in Settings > Staff & PINs.
                       Tip: write the owner PIN down somewhere safe,
                       or create a second owner account.
  No updates         - Is there internet? Windows 7 needs .NET 4.5 or
                       newer for secure downloads from GitHub. See
                       updates\update-log.txt.
  Slow laptop        - Close other programs. PicklePOS uses very
                       little memory; Chrome tabs and videos use much
                       more.
